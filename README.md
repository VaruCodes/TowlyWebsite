# Towly

### Roadside help, made simpler.

Towly is an early-stage business idea for an on-demand towing and roadside
assistance application. The goal is to make it easier for drivers to find
reliable towing services when they need help.

## The Idea

Towly would work similarly to ride-sharing apps, but instead of requesting
a ride, users would request towing or roadside assistance.

Users would be able to:

- Request a towing or roadside service
- Share their location
- Find nearby service providers
- View estimated pricing and arrival times
- Track their service
- Rate their experience

## Why Towly?

The idea came from my experience working around drivers and hearing about
how stressful it can be to find roadside assistance. I started wondering
whether there could be a simpler way to connect drivers with towing companies.

## Current Stage

Towly is currently in the early development and research stage. I am
researching the market, developing the business plan, exploring the brand,
and planning what an initial MVP could look like.
